    ❓ Help & Controls

    How to use the multiplayer platform

    Movement

    • WASD — Move · Mouse — Look · Escape — Release cursor

    Interaction

    • E — Interact · F — Hint · T — Teleport to objective

    Chat

    • Enter — Open chat input · Esc — Close chat

    Multiplayer

    • All users in the same browser session share a training room

    • Trainers see a toolbar to broadcast, pause, push hints & spawn markers

    • Admin sees full login history and audit log in Analytics

    Admin Access

    • Admin credentials are issued privately by your administrator

    • If you don't have credentials, please contact your training lead

    Emergency

    • Press the red Stop button to end the session safely
